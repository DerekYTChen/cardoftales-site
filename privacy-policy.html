Privacy Policy for CardofTales

Last updated: September 24, 2026

CardofTales ("the App," "we," "us") is a storytelling app for families and children. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, and how the one optional feature that sends data off your device works. We built the App to work entirely on your device wherever possible, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CardofTales does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or other account or contact information — the App has no account or sign-in system
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking of any kind
  • Photos, contacts, or other personal data from your device

CardofTales does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking technology. We do not track you or your device across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores your preferences and in-progress stories (such as selected cards, story answers, and app settings) locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is not accessible to us. Deleting the App removes this data.

3. Optional Feature: Google AI Mode Story Generation

CardofTales lets you build a story from cards and guided questions. When Apple Intelligence is unavailable on your device, or if you choose to use it, the App offers an optional, clearly user-initiated option to generate your story using Google Search's AI Mode instead.

If you choose this option, the story details you've entered in the App (such as your selected character, setting, goal, and any free-text answers you've written) are sent to Google as part of a search query, and the result is shown to you in an in-app browser view. This only happens when you actively choose this option — it is never sent automatically or in the background.

Because this feature sends your content to Google, that data becomes subject to Google's own privacy practices, not ours. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy before using this feature. We do not control, and are not responsible for, how Google handles data sent through this optional feature.

All other App features, including on-device story generation via Apple Intelligence when available, do not send your story content anywhere.

4. Children's Privacy

CardofTales is designed to be suitable for children and families. The App does not require or collect any account information, real name, contact information, or other personal information from any user, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your region).

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information to us in a manner not described here, please contact us using the information below and we will address it promptly.

Parents and guardians should be aware that the optional Google AI Mode feature described in Section 3 sends story content to Google if actively selected. We recommend parents review this feature with their children before use.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. The only third party involved in the App's operation is Google, and only when you explicitly choose the Google AI Mode story generation feature described in Section 3.

6. Data Security

Any network connection made by the App (for example, when using the optional Google AI Mode feature) uses standard HTTPS encryption. We do not use any custom or proprietary encryption, and we do not store your data on our own servers because we do not operate any servers that receive your data.

7. Your Choices

  • You can use CardofTales fully without ever using the Google AI Mode feature.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
