Frequently Asked Questions

How does CardofTales create stories?

You pick cards representing a character, setting, and goal, then answer a few guided questions. CardofTales assembles your answers into a story prompt and generates the story using on-device Apple Intelligence when available, or an optional cloud-based option you can choose yourself.

Does CardofTales work offline?

Card selection and question answering work fully offline. Story generation requires either on-device Apple Intelligence (if supported by your device) or an internet connection if you choose the optional cloud-based generation option.

Does CardofTales collect my personal information?

No. CardofTales does not require an account and does not collect personal information, track your activity, or show ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is CardofTales suitable for children?

Yes. CardofTales is designed for family and children's storytelling. Story generation choices, including any optional cloud-based feature, are visible and selectable by whoever is using the app, so parents can review these options with their children.

I found a bug or have a feature request. What should I do?

Please email us at derekyt.chen@icloud.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version if possible. Screenshots are always helpful.
